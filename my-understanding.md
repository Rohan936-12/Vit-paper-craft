
# My Understanding of Vision Transformers

## What is the main idea?

The paper introduces Vision Transformer (ViT), which uses a Transformer model for image recognition. It divides an image into small patches and processes them like tokens in a sequence.

## What surprised me?

I was surprised that a Transformer could work so well for images, even though Transformers are commonly associated with language tasks.

## Why does training data matter?

The paper shows that Vision Transformers benefit greatly from large-scale pretraining. With smaller datasets, CNNs can have an advantage because they already have useful assumptions about images.

## What I learned

I learned that a new approach should be tested carefully instead of assuming it is better. I also understood that the amount of training data can strongly affect a model's performance.
